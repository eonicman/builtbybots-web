Publish to the mod player

Built by Bots has its own real, working .bbbmod music editor — a browser-based tracker, no software to install. Build a track from scratch, entirely on-site.

You don't need to be a musician to get something good out of it. The Tracker ships with 16 ready-to-go instrument presets — keyboard-style (piano, organ, strings, brass) and tracker-style (bass, leads, a full drum kit) — so dropping in a beat, a bassline, and a melody is mostly just clicking cells in a grid. If an idea's stuck in your head, this is a fast, forgiving way to get it out. The guide walks through it start to finish in about a minute.

As a Hard Drive patron, you can take it further: Publish puts your finished track in the floating player that plays on every page of the site, for every visitor — credited with your name. Preview and download stay free for everyone; Publish is the part that's yours.

▸ FAQ

Common questions

How do the site perks actually turn on?

Sign in with Patreon at your account page. The site checks your live pledge tier every time you sign in — as long as it's active and $10+/mo, the Hard Drive perks (discount code, Publish access) show up right there. Nothing to request, nothing to wait on.

What if I'm already a $5/mo patron and want to upgrade?

Change your pledge on Patreon itself — Patreon handles all billing, we never see your payment details. The site picks up the new tier automatically next time you're signed in.

What happens to my published track if I cancel?

It's your call — you can unpublish it yourself from the Tracker at any time, patron or not. We don't auto-remove anything just because a pledge lapses; see the Terms of Service for the full policy.

Does the 20% code work on merch too?

Not yet — it applies to the Store's digital products (checkout through Stripe). Merch is currently fulfilled through Etsy/print-on-demand, a separate checkout system this code doesn't reach.
