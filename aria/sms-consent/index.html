SMS Consent & Opt-In — Aria

Last updated: August 2026

Aria (a product of QuickQuest Digital) sends text messages only to people who have explicitly agreed to receive them. This page describes exactly how that consent is collected, for two situations:

1. Callers to Aria's phone line

At the end of a call with Aria's AI voice receptionist (+1-855-272-4773), if a text follow-up is relevant (an appointment confirmation, a callback detail, a message summary), Aria asks the caller directly, out loud, before sending anything:

"Before we wrap up — would it be okay if we text you at this number? That could include a confirmation of what we just discussed, or a follow-up detail. Message and data rates may apply, and frequency depends on what's happening with your request. You can reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. Is that okay?"

Only if the caller says yes does Aria send anything to that number. Declining has no effect on the phone service itself.

2. Beta testers

Beta testers of Aria are asked directly, in a personal conversation or email, whether they're willing to receive occasional short text surveys about how Aria is working for them. Example:

"Would you mind if we checked in occasionally with a short text survey about how Aria's actually working for you? Reply STOP anytime to opt out — no hard feelings."

This is opt-in only, one beta tester at a time, based on an explicit yes from that person — never assumed, never bundled with using the product.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any text from Aria at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us at eon2010@gmail.com.

What we don't do

We don't sell, share, or rent phone numbers or opt-in information to third parties. See our full Privacy Policy for more.
