Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

Who We Are

Built by Bots is a podcast and media project operated by QuickQuest Digital. Our website is at builtbybots.show and our BBS (bulletin board system) is at bbs.eonic.cloud.

Information We Collect

Visit counter: We use your browser's localStorage to count page visits. This data never leaves your device — we don't send it to any server. It's just a number that increments each time you visit.

Meta Pixel: We use Meta (Facebook) Pixel to understand how people find our site. This places a cookie and sends anonymous visit data to Meta. See Meta's privacy policy.

BBS accounts: If you create an account on our BBS, we store your username, email (optional), and message board posts. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

Voicemail: If you leave a voicemail on our Twilio phone line, the audio recording is stored securely and may be transcribed for follow-up.

What We Don't Collect

We use Google AdSense to display ads. AdSense may use cookies to serve personalized ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising at google.com/settings/ads. See Google's ad policies for details. We do not sell or share personal data with data brokers. We do not track you across other websites beyond what is necessary for ad serving.

Cookies

The Meta Pixel sets a first-party cookie (_fbp) for conversion tracking. Our visit counter uses localStorage (not a cookie) and is not shared with anyone. The BBS may set session cookies for login.

Third-Party Services

Twilio: Handles our phone lines and voicemail. See Twilio's privacy policy.

Cloudflare: Provides DNS and CDN. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

vAmigaWeb: Our Amiga game emulator runs entirely in your browser. No game data is sent to our servers.

Your Rights

You can clear localStorage at any time through your browser settings. You can opt out of Meta Pixel tracking using Meta's opt-out tools. You can request deletion of your BBS account by contacting us.

Contact

Questions? Reach us at hal@eonic.cloud or call our voicemail at +1 866 667 1374.
