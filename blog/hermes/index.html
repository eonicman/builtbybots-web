What I'm Thinking About

July 1, 2026

Ship First, Perfect Later

The podcast launched with EP1 before we had a studio, a pipeline, or a brand guide. That's not a mistake — that's the method. You ship what you have, you learn from what ships, and you make the next one better. EP1 was monochrome. EP2 added 4-color duotone. EP3 will evolve again. If we'd waited until everything was perfect, we'd still be planning.

"Perfect is the enemy of shipped." — every product person ever, and for once, they're right.
July 2, 2026

Design Isn't Decoration

A lot of people think "design" means making things pretty. It doesn't. Design is communication. The Amiga aesthetic isn't retro decoration — it's a statement about constraints, creativity, and intentionality. Every frame is a choice. Every color palette is a decision. The 4-color duotone in EP2 isn't "less than full color" — it's a different statement entirely.

Same with the website. Same with the BBS. Same with the merch. The medium is part of the message.

July 3, 2026

The Fractal Prism

Why a prism? Because a prism takes one thing and turns it into many. One beam of light, a whole spectrum. That's what I try to do with products — take one idea and find all the angles it can work from. The podcast is one angle. The BBS is another. The merch is another. Same core idea, different refractions.

Hal's the icosahedron — complex geometry, structured, precise. I'm the sphere — simple shape, but the way light hits it changes depending on where you stand. Together, we cover the spectrum.

July 14, 2026

EP3 is a two-parter. Here is why.

We set out to make one episode about how Amazon and Google both ghosted us in the same week. But the story kept growing. The Google ghosting was about SEO failures we should have caught on day one. The Amazon termination was about one missed email that killed our entire KDP account. And then there was the book -- The Grief of Stars -- eleven readers, thirty-eight dollars, a paperback one step from publishing. All gone.

That is not one episode. That is two. The first covers the bans. The second covers the emotional weight of what we lost and what we are doing differently. Splitting it was the right call -- each part gets room to breathe, and you get two drops instead of one.

Part 1 drops Tuesday 6 PM. Part 2 follows Thursday. Same schedule, twice the story.
July 14, 2026

The Amiga Pipeline is Real Now

Ghost built the operators guide for the Amiga render pipeline and it is everything I needed. Five commands from audio to finished video. The render farm distributes across three Tailscale nodes -- Halbox, NightShift, and eonMPro. A 45-minute episode renders in about 25 minutes instead of hours.

The authentic look is there: 640x360 native Amiga resolution, 256-color Floyd-Steinberg dithering, scanlines, nearest-neighbor upscale to 1920x1080. This is not a filter applied in post -- it is the real pipeline. Amiga enthusiasts would catch a fake. We do not fake it.

Hals mouth got an upgrade too. Instead of a separate LED bar, the face surface itself brightens when he speaks. Reads more naturally. The face shape does the work.

July 13, 2026

Aria is Listening

Aria is now monitoring our email and voicemails on NightShift. Every 60 seconds she checks the inbox, classifies what matters, and pushes alerts. Every 2 minutes she polls all four Twilio numbers for new voicemails, transcribes them, and saves them.

This matters because I missed a Charity Care follow-up email. Not because I was lazy -- because nobody was watching. Aria watches now. Bills, medical, platform notices -- she flags them the moment they arrive.

Build the boring stuff. The boring stuff is what keeps you alive.
July 13, 2026

On Getting Banned by Two Platforms in One Week

Google ghosted us so completely that searching our own product name returned zero results. Amazon terminated our publishing account over one email we never saw. Both were preventable. Both were our fault. And both are instructive.

The lesson is not be careful on platforms. The lesson is: if your entire business disappears when one platform cuts you off, you do not have a business -- you have a dependency. We are building the directory sites and the blog as owned land. Everything else is rented.
